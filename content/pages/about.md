My name, Altansuvd, means golden pearl. I used to think it was just a pretty name. Now I think it was a forecast.

A pearl begins with something that hurts. Mine began with what I didn't have: a famous school, money, a map. Then came the dark part, the years no one saw. A new country. An alphabet I had only just learned. Seasons when I worked hard, felt behind, and had nothing to show for it.

Later, I did something a little strange. I gave every year of my twenties a score, and the numbers told me what my feelings couldn't: my lowest years for achievement were my highest for growth. I wasn't behind. I was forming.

That is what this place is about. The quiet years. The layers nobody claps for. The ordinary days that turn out to be the story.

I'm serious about most things, except myself. If you'd rather have one deep conversation than a hundred small ones, pull up a chair. The lamp is on.

## Why "the melo pearl"

This corner of the internet lives at *themelopearl*. Melo pearls are among the rarest pearls on earth: golden orange, with a soft glow like a flame. Nobody has ever managed to farm them. Each one grows naturally, in its own time.

That felt right.
