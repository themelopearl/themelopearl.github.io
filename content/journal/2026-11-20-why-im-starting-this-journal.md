For most of my twenties, I measured myself by what I could show. A scholarship. A test score. A new country in my passport. When there was something to show, I felt like I was moving. When there wasn't, I felt like I was falling behind everyone I knew.

It took me a long time to notice that the years with nothing to show were not empty. They were full of things that don't photograph well: learning a language from the alphabet up, starting over more than once, sitting with not knowing where I was going, and slowly becoming someone who could carry all of that.

My name, Altansuvd, means golden pearl. A pearl is what an oyster makes from the thing that hurts it, slowly, in the dark, one layer at a time. Nobody sees it forming. For years I thought that was just a pretty image. Now I think it's the most honest description of my twenties I have.

This journal is where I want to write about those layers. Not the polished version, the true one: the quiet years, the doors that opened right after I had decided to walk away, the ordinary days that turned out to be the story.

I'll write in English, Mongolian or Korean, depending on which language a thought arrives in. Some pages will be finished. Most will still be growing.

If you're in a quiet year of your own right now, I hope you find something here that makes you feel a little less alone, and a little less behind.

Would you be my friend on this road?
